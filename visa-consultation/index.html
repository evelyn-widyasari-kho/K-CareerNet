1. D-2 to E-7 Change of Status

The transition from student to professional requires careful planning to meet strict government requirements.

  • Job Offer Verification: We review your employment contract to ensure it meets the specific criteria for E-7 sponsorship.
  • Document Preparation: Detailed assistance with major-specific certification, degree verification, and company tax documents.
  • Eligibility Assessment: Personal review of your academic background to confirm it aligns with the 80+ recognized E-7 occupations.

2. Points-Based Residency (F-2-7)

The F-2-7 visa offers long-term stability and freedom of employment for high-earning and skilled professionals.

  • Score Calculation: Precise calculation of your points based on age, education, Korean proficiency (KIIP/TOPIK), and income.
  • Strategy Planning: Tailored advice on how to bridge the gap if you are a few points short of the 80-point requirement.
  • Extension Support: Guidance on maintaining your status and renewing your residency during income fluctuations.

3. KIIP & Language Requirements

The Korea Immigration & Integration Program (KIIP) is the most powerful tool for permanent residency and citizenship.

  • KIIP Pathway: Step-by-step guidance on how to skip levels using your TOPIK score to save time.
  • Point Maximization: Assistance in understanding how KIIP completion adds up to 28 critical points toward your F-2-7 visa.
  • Interview Prep: Mock interview sessions for those applying for F-5 permanent residency or naturalization.

4. Specialized Visa Solutions

Support for a wide range of specialized visas tailored to your unique situation in South Korea.

  • D-10 Job Seeker: Maximize your 6-month stay periods with professional guidance on internship reporting and point scoring.
  • E-9 to E-7-4: Helping skilled manual workers transition to professional status through the points-based system.
  • Family Support: Assistance with F-3 (Dependent) visas to ensure your family can stay with you in Korea.
