Korean Language Programs

  • Home /
  • Korean Language Programs

1. Regular Korean Course

Our Regular Course is designed for students seeking a consistent and immersive learning environment to master the language from the ground up.

  • Beginner (Level 1 - 2): Build a strong foundation in Hangeul, basic grammar, and essential vocabulary through the lens of Korean daily culture.
  • Intermediate (Level 3 - 4): A 4-week full immersion experience focusing on complex sentence structures and fluid communication in social settings.
  • Advanced (Level 5 - 6): Refine your proficiency to a professional level, enabling you to understand academic lectures and participate in high-level debates.

2. Korean Culture Program

Experience Korea beyond the classroom with programs that combine language learning with unique cultural adventures.

  • Korean Camp Program: Perfect for younger learners or those seeking fun, interactive activities like K-Pop dance, cooking, and traditional crafts.
  • Seasonal Immersions: Our Summer and Winter programs offer a 7-day experience in Seoul, featuring city tours and seasonal activities alongside language lessons.
  • Practical Experience: Gain firsthand knowledge of Korean etiquette and customs through guided cultural excursions.

3. Korean TOPIK Preparation

Achieve your certification goals with our specialized TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean) track, designed for maximum efficiency.

  • Proven Success: We maintain a 95% pass rate on the first attempt by focusing on the specific logic and patterns of the official exam.
  • Comprehensive Strategy: Includes intensive vocabulary building, listening comprehension drills, and specialized writing workshops for TOPIK II.
  • Mock Examinations: Receive personalized feedback through regular practice exams to track your progress over our 8-week intensive coaching period.

4. Business Korean Program

Specifically tailored for professionals and job-seekers looking to navigate the corporate landscape in South Korea.

  • Corporate Communication: Master the art of professional presentations, corporate terminology, and formal honorifics used in the workplace.
  • Business Etiquette: Learn the nuances of Korean meeting protocols, contract negotiations, and professional email communication.
  • Personalized Attention: Class sizes are strictly limited to 6 students to ensure intensive, high-quality interaction with our expert instructors.
