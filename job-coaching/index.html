Professional Job Coaching

  • Home /
  • Professional Job Coaching

1. Resume & Portfolio Review

Navigating the Korean job market requires a specific approach to your application documents.

  • Korean-Style Resume (Sogyeoseo): We help you craft professional resumes that meet the expectations of Korean HR managers.
  • Portfolio Optimization: Align your previous experience with the specific needs of the Korean tech and business industries.
  • Personal Branding: Develop a unique value proposition that highlights your strengths as a global talent in Korea.

2. Interview Preparation

Mastering the interview process is the final step to securing your dream job in Seoul or beyond.

  • Mock Interviews: Participate in realistic interview simulations with feedback on your body language and speaking style.
  • Cultural Nuances: Learn the essential business etiquette and honorifics required for formal corporate interviews.
  • Q&A Strategy: Prepare high-impact answers for common questions regarding your visa status and long-term commitment to Korea.

3. Career Path Strategy

Whether you are a fresh graduate or an experienced professional, we provide a roadmap for your growth.

  • Industry Analysis: Gain insights into which sectors in Korea are currently hiring international professionals.
  • Networking Skills: Learn how to leverage platforms like LinkedIn and local networking events to find “hidden” job openings.
  • Salary Negotiation: We provide data-driven advice to help you negotiate fair compensation and benefit packages.

4. University to Career Transition

Specialized support for international students looking to change their D-2 visa to an E-7 or F-series professional visa.

  • Visa Alignment: Ensure your job offer meets the strict government requirements for visa sponsorship.
  • Internship Placement: Assistance in finding internship opportunities that can lead to full-time employment.
  • Corporate Culture: Workshops on surviving and thriving in the unique “Palli-Palli” culture of Korean workplaces.
